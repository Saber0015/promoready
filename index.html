Before
the first cut.

A few things to make the brief easier.

What if I only have a few product photos?

That is a useful place to start. Share what you have and tell us the message. We review the material and propose a workable direction before confirming the quote.

Are the sample films your client projects?

They are supplied style references for this concept. They show visual directions and pacing, and do not establish a client relationship or production ownership. Original brands and credits are retained.

Will the video show my actual product?

We work from the material you supply and keep product appearance and claims accurate. If a generated scene or other visual treatment would help, we discuss it with you before production.

How long does delivery take?

We confirm timing with your quote after reviewing your material and scope. You can include a preferred date in the brief, and we will tell you whether it is workable.

Can I request changes or an extra format?

The draft packages include one round of minor revisions and one publishing ratio per video. A new concept, significant reshoot, or extra version is scoped and quoted separately.

What do I need to share or have permission to use?

Share a link to the material rather than uploading it here. Please use photos, clips, logos, music and appearances you are entitled to use. We confirm any additional music or media choices before the final edit.
